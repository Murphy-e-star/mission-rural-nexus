01 / WHO WE ARE

Building stronger rural futures, together.

Mission Rural Nexus is a community-based organisation focused on sustainable agriculture, youth participation, climate resilience and rural development.

We believe the people closest to a challenge should help shape its solutions. MRN creates space for farmers, youth groups, community organisations and partners to exchange knowledge, build capacity and act on local priorities.
